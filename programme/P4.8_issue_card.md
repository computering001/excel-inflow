## Difference classification
- Intended correction: a reader of a SOLVED artifact whose expectation is the case and nothing else. `Recomputation` derives every exogenous input from declared data — the operating drivers from the broker pack under the case's own `broker_case` control (`Consensus` and `Model Consensus` are the same authority: the mean of the houses the declared `consensus_membership` admits per period) or from `operating_metrics` where the forecast tail carries numbers; the effective tax rate from the same authority with an explicit `absent` state so a silent 0% cannot pass as a resolved 0%; the minimum-cash floor from `minimum_cash_override` or the lowest historical year-end cash; every instrument's seven movement terms from its own declared terms, day-count, maturity and FX; the lease from `lease_policy`; and the income statement from the case's declared `statement_structure.income_statement`, evaluated as data where a role is a supplied input row and as its declared calculation otherwise. `fixed_point()` then iterates the cash / interest / revolver circularity by Gauss-Seidel over the whole forecast to a residual of 1e-12 — four orders tighter than the solver's declared 1e-8 — and `compare()` reports a typed finding for every disagreement across seven domains (`convergence`, `rcf_draw_repay`, `debt_roll_forward`, `opening_debt`, `effective_tax_rate`, `typed_states`, `workbook_cross_read`). The revolver sweep is DERIVED FROM CASH NEED: the draw is the period's deficit against the declared floor capped by declared headroom, the repayment is the surplus capped by the opening balance, and a draw with cash above the floor (or a repayment with cash below it) is a typed finding the roll-forward identity structurally cannot see. Non-self-confirmation is machine-checkable rather than asserted: `expectation_digest` is a sha256 over the COMPLETE recomputation — every field of every period of every declared branch — so it is a pure function of the case, and the suite proves it is byte-identical across all 123 mutations including one that rescales every solved number in the artifact by 1.01. Checks whose two sides both come from the artifact (the seven-term identity over the solver's own typed terms, the revolver roll-forward, period-n opening against period-(n−1) ending) go through a separate `identity()` path that is deliberately excluded from the digest and labelled `expectation_source: the artifact's own terms`.
