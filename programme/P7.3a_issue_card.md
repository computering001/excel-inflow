## The design decision, and why

`interesting_seeds.length > 0` had three possible resolutions once every product defect was repaired.

1. **Delete the assertion.** Rejected. It is the only check that the registry — whose entire reason to exist is that a failing seed can be persisted and replayed by seed alone — actually holds anything. Deleting it would make the registry unfalsifiable exactly when it stopped being exercised.
2. **Keep or invent a product defect to feed the counter.** Rejected outright. A pin past its repair asserts a defect that no longer exists, which is a false claim in the opposite direction from a loosened property, and an invented one is simply a lie in an artifact.
3. **Prove the MECHANISM with the synthetic oracle that already exists.** Adopted.

The `shrink_proof_oracle` (`scripts/lib/case_generator.mjs`, "Synthetic oracle used only to prove the shrinker reduces and preserves a failure signature; it is never a product claim") is the device this space already declared for precisely this eventuality: "the shrinker is proved against a synthetic oracle … so the proof survives the repair of every real defect below". Reusing it for the registry is consistent with standing doctrine rather than a new invention, and it needs no new machinery: the synthetic entry is found in the same cohort sweep, keyed by the same signature machinery, replayed by the same seed-only `generateCase({ seed, context })` path and shrunk by the same shrinker as a product entry — so what it proves is the identical mechanism. What it does NOT do is claim anything about the product: it is labelled `provenance: "synthetic_mechanism_proof"`, `severity: "not_a_defect"`, carries a `not_a_product_claim` statement in the artifact, and is excluded from every violation count.

The truthful state — zero product defects — is then representable, and option (b)'s strengthening is taken as well rather than instead: the product section may be empty, but that emptiness is DECLARED and cross-checked, and every entry is held to a strictly stronger standard than before.

Net effect on strength (nothing was weakened):

- kept verbatim: `Array.isArray(interesting_seeds) && length > 0`
- kept at exact strength: `replayed.sha256 === entry.case_sha256`
- ADDED: every entry must declare a typed provenance (`product_property` | `synthetic_mechanism_proof`)
- ADDED: at least one synthetic entry must exist AND must be the declared oracle signature and property id — a product signature cannot wear a synthetic label, and a synthetic label cannot be swapped for an invented defect
- ADDED: every entry must still RAISE the signature it was persisted with (probe mode only for synthetic entries; the product battery never runs in probe mode) — the registry-level form of the retirement doctrine, so a byte-identical replay of a case that stopped failing no longer passes
- ADDED: the sweep's declared product-violation count and the persisted product entries must agree, so an empty product section is a checked fact and not a silence
- ADDED: every `retired_dispositions` row must name the layer and the commit that repaired it, and may not still stand in the dispositioned set — a retirement has to be justified in the artifact, not merely performed
