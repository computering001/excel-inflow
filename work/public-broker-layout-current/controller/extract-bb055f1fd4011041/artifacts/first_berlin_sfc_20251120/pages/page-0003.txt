20 November 2025                                                                                SFC Energy AG





In Q3, Clean Power Management segment revenue fell 21% y/y to €9.3m. This is mainly
attributable to the loss of a single project (value: €2.8m) in Canada to a competitor. The
gross margin declined almost 5 PP to 24.9%. We note that on a nine month basis, the
segment gross margin rose almost 3 PP to 29.7% based on higher product prices. Adjusted
Q3 segment EBITDA contracted 66% y/y to €0.5m.


Balance sheet burdened by higher working capital requirement  In the first nine months,
working capital increased 41% to €72m, pushing the working capital ratio (working capital /
revenue of the last four quarters) above 50% (see figure 3). While the high inventory stems
mainly from an overly optimistic revenue plan, higher trade receivables are traced to
customers paying their bills later (days sales outstanding: 114 versus 90 at the end of 2024).
A very high equity ratio of almost 74% and a net cash position of €25m show that the
balance sheet is  still strong. We expect SFC to succeed in reducing the working capital
requirement to a normal level within the next six months based on a more cautious revenue
plan and more rigorous invoice collection.

High operating cash outflow  Operating cash flow amounted to €-12.4m due to a higher
working capital requirement. CapEx of €-2.9m yielded free cash flow of €-15.3m. Financing
cash flow was negative (€-2.8m) due mainly to the repayment of lease liabilities. Net cash
flow amounted to €-17.8m (see figure 4).
