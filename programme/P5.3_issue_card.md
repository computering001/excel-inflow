## Difference classification
- Intended correction: the provenance claim now NAMES what it is a claim about, and a reader that shares no code with the emitter resolves it. Three parts. (1) BINDING — every provenance-marked statement cell records a POINTER to the authority record governing it (`{kind, row_id, period_index, forecast_index}`), and every comment that asserts a source records the record it was rendered from; both travel in `<workbook>.provenance-authority.json`, deliberately NOT inside the plan, because `assets/plan.schema.json` closes the plan and the emitted `.xlsx` must not move by one byte for carrying a binding. The binding is a pointer and never a copy: an emitter that copied the authority's contents into its own binding could make a wrong colour agree with a convenient story. (2) EMITTER REFUSAL — `refuseContradictedProvenance()` runs after the last styling act and throws rather than shipping a lie, on two grounds: mechanically over EVERY sheet (blue over a formula, a cross-sheet formula not marked green, green over a same-sheet formula — white remains the chrome channel `releaseFormulaProvenance` documents, which is what `R15 = I15` is), and authority-bound over the bound cells (a row the case declares derived may not ship as a blue hardcode). It repaints nothing. (3) ORACLE — `verify/provenance_authority_oracle.py` takes its OBSERVATION from the emitted package (P5.4's `reconstruct_plan`, so resolved font colours, formulas and every comment's cell and text come out of the `.xlsx` bytes) and its EXPECTATION from the AUTHORITY RECORDS: the case's own row declaration and `provenance` ledger for historical periods, and `model-ir-v3.planes.authority` (`producer_type`, `method`, `source_kind`, `formula_operator`) for forecast periods. A hand-authored table says what each authority PERMITS the mark to say — `BrokerInput` reaches the statement as a cross-sheet link and may not be a hardcode; `Derived` and `ScheduleLink` may not be blue; a `UserInput` that declares a formula operator is a formula and one that does not is an entry or a link; `CapturedBy` holds nothing. Comment TRUTH is checked by re-rendering the comment the authority record entails and comparing the whole text, so a fabricated document, page, publication date, label, unit or transformation is a typed finding; a `Forecast authority:` note is checked against the authority plane's `method` and `source_kind`; a filed-source citation on a cell the case declares computed is a finding even when every field of it renders faithfully; a blue historical hardcode that cites no page is a finding; and a source-asserting comment nobody bound is a finding, so the channel cannot be escaped by writing a claim outside the grid. The emitter's pointers are not taken on trust — each is re-derived from the row map's geometry and refused on disagreement, and a binding that records a colour the file does not carry is `PROV_BINDING_MARK_DRIFT`.
