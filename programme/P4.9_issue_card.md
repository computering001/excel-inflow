## Difference classification

- Intended correction:
  - **D30 — the acceptance test is now proportional to the state, floored by IEEE754, and capped by a DECLARED envelope.**
    `residual <= min( max(rho * S, nu(state)), ceiling )`, where `S` is the L-infinity magnitude of the state vector across the two iterates being compared, `nu` is the recursive-summation float-noise bound `(n-1) * u * SUM|xi| + u * S` with `u = 2^-53` and the same safety factor of 4 that P2.10 derived for the footing oracle — so the repository's two tolerance homes agree about what doubles can resolve — and `ceiling` is `policy.solver.absolute_tolerance`. `rho` is that same tolerance restated at a reference state magnitude of 10^3 reporting units (`rho = absolute_tolerance / REFERENCE_STATE_MAGNITUDE = 1e-11`), so the criterion reproduces 1e-8 EXACTLY at S = 10^3 and is strictly stricter below it.
  - **The direction that was repaired is the one with teeth.** The register warned that simply widening the absolute number blinds the solver at SMALL scale, and that is precisely what the constant was doing: a state vector of magnitude ~1 moving 5e-9 per sweep — a fifth of its own resolution requirement — was accepted as a fixed point. The suite pins that it is now refused. Both terms of the criterion are proportional to the state, so inside the envelope the verdict is exactly scale-free: the suite proves a thousand-times-smaller issuer takes the identical number of sweeps and is judged at exactly a thousandth of the tolerance.
  - **The envelope is DECLARED, not hidden.** Above S = 10^3 the scale-free criterion would exceed `absolute_tolerance`, and three readers outside this package's mandate refuse that — measured, not assumed (see Unresolved). So the ceiling binds there, and every period it decides carries `within_declared_envelope: false` with `binding_term: "declared_absolute_ceiling"` and the uncapped `scale_free_tolerance` beside it, so a reader can see exactly where scale-freeness stops and by how much. This is the register's own second permitted resolution — declare it scale-dependent and narrow the envelope — applied to the half that could not move alone. Nothing was widened: the criterion is stricter than the retired constant everywhere and never looser.
