Risk

The chart displays the distribution of daily returns of Gerresheimer AG over the last
3 years, compared to the same distribution for West Pharmaceutical Services, Inc..
We have also included the distribution for the index Germany DAX (TR). The
distribution gives a better understanding of risk than measures like volatility, which
assume that log returns are normally distributed. In reality, they are skewed (down
moves are larger) and have fat tails (large moves occur more often than predicted).
Also, volatility treats up and down moves the same, while investors are more worried
about down moves. For Gerresheimer AG, the worst day during the past 3 years was
11/02/2026 with a share price decline of -31.4%. The best day was 20/03/2026
when the share price increased by 21.8%.
