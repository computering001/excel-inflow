Company Background

 Smartbroker's business model is divided into the media business and the transaction business, which is bundled in Smartbroker AG.

 In the media business, Smartbroker operates the financial information platforms wallstreet-online, boersennews, finanznachrichten and
 ariva and are the number one in Germany with 2.5bn page views and 1m registered users.

 In the transaction business segment, the company offers a wide range of services in the field of online securities trading with its new
 SMARTBROKER+ platform.

 The company has successfully positioned itself as a hybrid of a neobroker and a traditional online broker and has diversified its value
 chain along financial services and capital markets.

 The attractive one-stop shopping option for customers should enable Smartbroker to achieve the strong customer growth seen in 2025
 (WRe + 76k new customers) in subsequent years as well.



Competitive Quality

 Smartbroker is well positioned in the German market as a ‘best of both worlds’ provider. Its greatest strengths lie in its attractive
 combination of a full product range and low prices.

 The SMARTBROKER + platform, newly launched in 2024, has significantly increased growth opportunities in transaction-based
 business.

 The marketing strategy, which was intensified in 2025, is very successful with average marketing costs per customer of ~115 EUR and
  is supported by consistent further development of the product range.

 Focusing on user experience and marketing should put Smartbroker on a par with leading neobrokers in order to make the most of the
 huge growth potential in retail brokerage.

 Diversified revenues from the media and transaction business segments, as well as rising recurring revenues, should reduce
 dependence on PFOF revenues and lower the cyclicality of the business model.
