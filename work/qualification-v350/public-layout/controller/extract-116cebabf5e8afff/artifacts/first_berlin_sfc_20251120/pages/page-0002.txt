20 November 2025                                                                                SFC Energy AG



Q3 significantly weaker y/y Revenue retreated 15% y/y to €29.1m due to weaker demand
in both segments. The gross profit dove 29% to €10.2m, and the gross margin retreated by
almost 7 PP to 35.2%, suppressed by negative exchange rate effects, tariffs, and a worse
product mix. Adjusted EBIT declined 91% to €0.4m, burdened by higher IT costs (new ERP
software) and higher capitalised R&D costs. Higher interest and tax expenses led to a net
loss of €-1.5m.

Both segments reported significantly weaker figures in Q3 The Clean Energy segment
suffered from weaker than expected demand from new clients in the US and lower defence
business. Segment revenue thus declined 12% y/y to €19.8m. The gross margin fell by 9 PP
to 40% due to adverse exchange rates & tariffs and a worse product mix. Adjusted segment
EBITDA retreated 58% y/y to €1.8m (see figure 2).
