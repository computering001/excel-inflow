 With new customer growth of 77k (previous year: 76k), Smartbroker fell slightly short of its revised guidance of 80k. However, this represents a clear
 improvement compared with new customer acquisition in 2024 (14k) and, in our view, demonstrates the acceptance of the smartbroker+ platform.
 Management expects this trend to continue in 2026, with new customer growth of ~100k. The average CAC in 2025 was approximately EUR 129 per
 new customer.
 Guidance: For 2026, management expects sales to move sideways in a range of EUR 67m–72m (WRe: 70.9m). This already takes the expected effects
  of the discontinuation of payment‑for‑order‑flow (PFOF) into account from July 2026. However, no changes are planned for end-customers. After 9M/25,
 these payments accounted for approximately 15% of total revenues.
 Management expects EBITDA to range from EUR –1.5m to EUR +1.5m. The discontinuation of PFOF payments is expected to be almost completely
  offset by cost‑cutting measures already initiated. Rising marketing costs of EUR 12.5m (EUR +2.5m vs 2025) have also been taken into account. The
 goal is to further improve adjusted EBITDA before CAC.
 In the portal business (wallstreet‑online, finanznachrichten.de, etc.), revenue of EUR 28m and EBITDA of EUR 6m are expected in 2026. The resulting
 EBITDA margin of 21% would be higher than in 2024 (19%), but probably lower than the, still unreported, 2025 margin (WRe: 25%). However, in our
  view, it would continue to deliver a very stable contribution to earnings and cash flow.
 In terms of new customer acquisition, management is counting on a further acceleration from 77k to 100k in 2026. To this end, the marketing budget is
 planned to increase by EUR 2.5m to EUR 12.5m, which corresponds to an average CAC per customer of EUR 125. Further investments are planned in
 products and technology, reflecting the increased focus on trading‑savvy customers who could generate additional revenue growth. For example, there
 are plans to expand the IT team by 20 positions with a focus on AI and trader needs (e.g. optimised desktop version, junior accounts). This is expected
  to result in a further increase in new customers to 130k by 2027 (previously 100k). The 13k new customers acquired in January and February, together
  with a record month of trades, support the targeted growth path.
 Conclusion: The targets for 2026 are ambitious, but the company has already significantly exceeded its original targets in 2025. The clear customer
 focus and technological development should support growth, suggesting that our previous estimates should be achievable. Possible effects from the
 implementation of a product for retirement provision, “Altersvorsorgedepot”, could further strengthen sales and customer growth. We confirm our Buy
 recommendation with a price target of EUR 18.60.
