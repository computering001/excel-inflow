20 November 2025                                                                                SFC Energy AG



Oneberry takeover would result in strong external growth and a strengthening of the
business model    Following the October  acquisition  of a 15% stake  in Oneberry
Technologies in Singapore (purchase price: lower single-digit million euros), SFC has an
option to acquire 50%. We expect a decision on the deal within the next five months.
Oneberry has a “security-as-a-service” business model, which, according to management, is
more profitable than SFC’s business. Oneberry’s long-term contracts with the Singapore
government generate most of the revenue of around €20m p.a. If SFC acquires a majority
stake in Oneberry,  it could expand the business model to other Asian countries such as
Indonesia, Malaysia, Thailand and the Philippines to give  its Asian business the long-
awaited push. Furthermore, the “security-as-a-service” business model could be offered in
other regions (Europe, US) as large customers signal interest.

Order intake and backlog show first signs of recovery  The 9M order intake declined
21% y/y to €78.4m, but Q3 saw the highest sequential order intake this year (€34.7m, Q2:
€26.0m, Q1: €17.7m) and grew 77% y/y. The order backlog rose 5% y/y to €79.3m. We note
that 40% - 50% of the orders are realised within a quarter and are thus not recorded in the
order backlog.

2025 guidance narrowed    Following the weak Q3  figures, SFC narrowed revenue
guidance towards the lower end of the range, and AEBIT & AEBITDA guidance towards the
lower half of the range given in July (see figure 5).

Forecasts lowered for 2025E and the following years  Given the current challenging
business environment we have lowered our forecasts for 2025E and the following years. We
expect continued growth in the US which will be supported by the ramp-up of the local US
production site. We are now factoring in 5% organic topline growth and an AEBIT margin of
5.0% for 2026E (see figure 6). We expect a return to double-digit growth in 2027 based on a
rebound in the defence business. Our forecasts do not incorporate external growth.

Buy rating confirmed at lower price target  An updated DCF model, which incorporates
the lowered forecasts, yields a new price target of €21 (previously: €26). We confirm our Buy
recommendation.
