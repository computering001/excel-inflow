## Difference classification

- Intended correction:
  - **D5 — both oracles made STRICT, not both made loose.** The register's hypothesis (the epsilon form is right, exact-0 is the bug) is only half correct, and the missing half is a real weakening. Verified: at a printed total of 500,000,000 with precision 0, `1e-9 * 5e8` exactly DOUBLED the half-unit rounding allowance to a full unit, so the footing pass PASSED a genuine one-printed-unit break. The exact-0 form was also wrong: 12.3 + 45.6 is 57.900000000000006, so zero tolerance rejects arithmetic correct to every printed digit. The resolution is therefore the one the brief names as correct if unification would loosen the stricter checker — fix the arithmetic comparison. The single tolerance is `roundingTolerance(precision) + floatNoiseTolerance({target, terms})`, where the noise term is the textbook recursive-summation bound `(n-1) * u * Σ|xᵢ| + u * |target|` (u = 2⁻⁵³) times a safety factor of 4. It is derived from IEEE754, not chosen: ~4.5 million times tighter than the `1e-9` form for an ordinary statement family, and non-zero exactly where doubles genuinely cannot resolve the difference.
  - **D6 — printed-but-unclassified is separated from not-printed.** `filedCellAssertion` sorts a filed cell into `value_bearing` / `declared_absent` / `unclassified` / `contradiction`. A DECLARED absence (`reported_blank`, `not_applicable`, or a raw `""`, which is what `case_compiler.typedHistoricalStates` itself reads as a printed blank) still asserts nothing and is still skipped in silence — no finding, no refusal, and above all no zero. Everything else raises `STATEMENT_FAMILY_UNCLASSIFIED_FILED_CELL` with a typed reason: `printed_glyph_unclassified` (state `reported_dash`/`unresolved` — the glyph is recorded, its numeric meaning is not), `value_bearing_state_over_empty_cell` (the classification claims a number the cell does not carry), or `no_declared_classification` (nothing was said at all). Material → blocker, immaterial → warning, matching the region's existing split. The finding deliberately carries NO `filed`/`members_sum` field: the refusal asserts nothing about the cell's value, so no zero is ever minted.
