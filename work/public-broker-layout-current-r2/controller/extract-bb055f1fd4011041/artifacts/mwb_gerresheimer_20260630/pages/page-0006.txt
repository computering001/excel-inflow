Valuation

 DCF Model

 The DCF model results in a fair value of EUR 12.43 per share:

 Top-line growth: We expect Gerresheimer AG to grow revenues at a CAGR of 2.1%
 between 2026E and 2033E. The long-term growth rate is set at 2.0%.

 ROCE. Returns on capital are developing from 2.8% in 2026E to 6.7% in 2033E.

 WACC. Starting point is an equity beta of 2.40. Unleverering and correcting for mean
 reversion yields an asset beta of 1.11. Combined with a risk-free rate of 2.0% and an
 equity risk premium of 6.0% this yields cost of equity of 15.7%. With pre-tax cost of
 borrowing at 5.0%, a tax rate of 30% and target debt/equity of 1.5 this results in a
 long-term WACC of 8.4%.
