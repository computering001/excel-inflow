CEO: Dr. Peter Podesser                                        CFO: Daniel Saxena                                                       Brunnthal
SFC Energy AG is a leading supplier of hydrogen and methanol fuel cells. In its Clean Energy & Clean Power Management business sectors, the company offers
products, systems and solutions for stationary and mobile off-grid power supply. Headquartered in Brunnthal near Munich, SFC Energy operates further produc­
tion sites in the Netherlands, Romania and Canada.
