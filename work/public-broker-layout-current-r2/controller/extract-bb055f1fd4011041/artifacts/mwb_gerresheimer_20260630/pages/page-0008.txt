Peer group analysis

A peer group or comparable company (“comps”) analysis is a methodology that
calculates a company's relative value — how much it should be worth based on how
 it compares to other similar companies. Given that Gerresheimer AG differs quite
significantly in terms of size, focus, financial health and growth trajectory, we regard
our peer group analysis merely as a support for other valuation methods. The peer
group of Gerresheimer AG consists of the stocks displayed in the graphs below. As
of 30 June 2026 the median market cap of the peer group was EUR 37,436m,
compared to EUR 950m for Gerresheimer AG. In the period under review, the peer
group was more profitable than Gerresheimer AG. The expectations for sales
growth are higher for the peer group than for Gerresheimer AG.
